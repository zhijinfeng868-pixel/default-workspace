The notation 𝑓 ∶ 𝐴 → 𝐵 means that 𝐴 is the domain of the function 𝑓 and 𝐵 is the codomain
of the function 𝑓.

If 𝑓 ∶ 𝐴 → 𝐵 then 𝑓(𝑥) ∈ 𝐵 for each 𝑥 ∈ 𝐴 .

the value of the function at 𝑥.
